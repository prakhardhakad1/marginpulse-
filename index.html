Why Forensic Auditing Wins Case Competitions

In competitions like SFU DataJam 2026, judges from Statistics Canada, SFU, and retail leaders immediately penalize teams that run naive demand regressions on raw transaction logs.

1. Unestimable Price Elasticity Guard

SKUs `FOODS_1_004` ($2.98) and `HOBBIES_2_031` ($4.97) have constant historical prices (`distinct_prices = 1`). Any regression outputting an elasticity for these items is fitting pure noise. MarginPulse flags them as `UNESTIMABLE` and locks their markdown tier to `0%`.

2. Stockout Censoring Bias Correction

When an item stocks out for 6 consecutive days at `CA_1` or `TX_2`, recorded sales drop to zero even at full demand. Training on censored zeroes biases $\hat{\epsilon}_{ii}$ toward zero. `elasticity.py` filters out `stockout_censored == 1` windows before fitting the log-log panel model.

3. Intermittent & Lumpy Tail Protection

50 of the 120 series fall into `Intermittent` ($ ext{ADI} \ge 1.32, ext{CV}^2 < 0.49$) or `Lumpy` ($ ext{ADI} \ge 1.32, ext{CV}^2 \ge 0.49$) regimes, evaluated via M5 revenue-weighted WRMSSE rather than skewed MAPE.

Econometric & MILP Mathematical Specification

1. Ridge Log-Log Panel Regression with Block Bootstrap

log(Q_{i,s,t} + 1) = α_s + ε_{ii} log(P_{i,s,t}) + ε_{ij} log(P̄_{-i,d,t}) + γ_1 Wknd_t + γ_2 SNAP_{s,t} + u_{i,s,t}

Fitted on uncensored training days ($t \le 84$) with store fixed effects $lpha_s$ and 100-iteration weekly block bootstrap to preserve autocorrelation when constructing 95% confidence intervals $[\epsilon_{2.5\%}, \epsilon_{97.5\%}]$.

2. Mixed-Integer Linear Program (`scipy.optimize.milp`)

max Σ_s [ Σ_m GM_{s,m} y_{s,m} - c_tr (t_s^in + t_s^out)/2 - p_short Short_s - h_cost EndInv_s ]

  • Σ_m y_{s,m} = 1: Binary markdown tier $m \in \{0\%, 10\%, 20\%, 30\%\}$.
  • I_0 + t_in - t_out - Σ_m D_{s,m} y_{s,m} + Short - EndInv = 0: Exact 4-week inventory conservation.
  • Σ_{s ∈ (i, state)} (t_in - t_out) = 0: Zero net creation of stock within `CA`, `TX`, and `WI`.
  • Σ_{s,m} Spend_{s,m} y_{s,m} ≤ B_cap: Global markdown budget cap.
